DES3322 - FURNITURE DESIGN : PRACTICE

THOMAS OLIPHANT

INDIVIDUAL

Furniture is the most democratic form of Architecture. It is for all and is at a level that can be created by all. It emphasizes much more of the design process than the final product, teaching through direct construction, a method of design in a field that doesn’t suffer the downfalls of bureaucratic “Perfection”.

Construction is an essential aspect of architecture, it’s about the core of how something comes together. What are the techniques that will get something to stand up? A skyscraper, a house, a chair? Observing how much precision can go into a well crafted product, and building.

Furniture at its core is a small form of architecture, spatial limitations must be kept in mind and constantly brought up against. But so must its cultural significance. Like any architecture, there is a motif, something to challenge and something it stands for.

In multiple prototypes the design process became a tornado of returning realizations and future thoughts. Broken experiments, transforming prototypes, rough maquettes helped me arrive at two places for Sitting.
